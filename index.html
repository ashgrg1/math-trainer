Core formulas

Days supply = total qty ÷ (dose × times/day)
mg/kg dose = mg/kg × weight in kg
% strength (w/v) = g ÷ 100 mL
Dilution = C₁ × V₁ = C₂ × V₂
IV flow = volume ÷ time (hr) → mL/hr
IV drip = (volume × drop factor) ÷ time (min)
Markup = cost + (cost × markup %)

Conversions to memorize

1 g = 1000 mg = 1,000,000 mcg
1 kg = 2.2 lb  ·  1 lb = 454 g
1 tsp = 5 mL  ·  1 tbsp = 15 mL
1 fl oz = 30 mL  ·  1 cup = 240 mL
1 gr (grain) = 65 mg
℞ A few every day beats one long cram — the methods stick through repetition
